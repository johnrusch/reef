<objective>
Wire AI enrichment output into the PlantUML generator so that AI-provided names, technologies, and relationships appear in rendered diagrams — fixing the `_enrichedData` discard bug.

Purpose: Currently all four generator methods prefix their `enrichedData` parameter with `_`, silently discarding the AI response. The diagrams are built entirely from static analysis heuristics. This plan makes the generator consume the structured enrichment data from Plan 12-01, using static analysis as a fallback only when AI data is empty.

Output: Updated PlantUML generator consuming typed enrichment data, updated analyzer service call site, and integration tests proving AI content appears in rendered PlantUML.
</objective>
